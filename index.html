Leitz Label Studio

Design and bulk-print labels for your Leitz Icon — fast, offline, and right from your phone.

Open the app → or add it to your Home Screen for offline use

What it does

🏷️

True-to-size labels

Continuous & die-cut cartridges at the Icon's native 300 dpi — prints 1:1.

🔳

QR & barcodes

Links, Wi-Fi, contact cards, Code 128 — with error-correction and centre logos.

▤

Bulk & CSV

Sequences, CSV import with column mapping, per-row serials and copies.

🎨

Design tools

Fonts, per-line sizes, symbols, borders, logos, inverse mode and tape colours.

⛶

Scan & verify

Check a printed label scans, capture scans to CSV, or import a shared design.

📄

Export anywhere

PDF, PNG, vector SVG, or an A4/Letter sheet for an ordinary printer.

Install it in seconds

  1. Tap Open the app above (or open this page in Safari on iPhone).
  2. Tap the Share icon → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Launch Label Studio — it runs full-screen and works offline.
🔒 Everything stays on your device — nothing you type is uploaded. Print from the app to the official Leitz Icon software at 100% / actual size.
